One agent. A hundred and fifty ways to shape a plot.

DesignAgent is an AI landscape designer built for NoblePort properties. It reads your plot — soil, sun, slope, setbacks — and answers with real design work: zone diagrams, planting palettes, grading plans, and the schedules to build them. Every capability is a named skill you can invoke directly.

Full project arc

From a rough plot to a working plan, in three moves

You never have to know which of the 150 skills to reach for — describe the plot and the goal, and DesignAgent routes the work. Or call a skill by name when you know exactly what you need.

STEP 01

Describe your plot

Address, survey, photos, or a sketch on a napkin. DesignAgent builds a base map of existing conditions — boundaries, levels, trees, services.

STEP 02

Set the brief

“Low-water front garden with a gravel court.” The agent selects and sequences the right skills — analysis first, then layout, planting, and detail.

STEP 03

Get buildable output

Scaled drawings, plant schedules with quantities, phased budgets, and contractor-ready briefs — not just pretty pictures.
